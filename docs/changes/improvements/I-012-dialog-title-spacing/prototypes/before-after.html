Before is the app today. After: the space between the title and the content is the same as between the content and the buttons, and no bigger than the pop-up's outer padding: 20 px on the desktop, 16 px on the phone. The outer padding stays as it is. Every value is even. The pink bands mark both spaces; the numbers are measured in the app.

Why: since the scroll area of I-004 went around the content, Mantine adds the content's 20 px top padding back. On the phone the title block keeps Mantine's 60 px minimum height around a 44 px close button.
